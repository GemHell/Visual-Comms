Reflection #2: AI Slop

For my thesis project, I propose an interactive environment that examines the increasing influence digital spaces have over our daily lives. For better or worse, social media platforms have become the primary arenas where societies negotiate politics, identity, and social norms. In the past, psychologists, anthropologists, and philosophers have theorized the ways in which physical spaces influence the development of societies and culture. Philosopher Jeremy Bentham famously used the panopticon as an example of how architectural spaces communicate ideas of power and surveillance to inhabitants; later, Foucault expanded on this, framing the Panopticon as a metaphor for the disciplinary power present in society at large. How, then, do we understand the way digital spaces affect us?
