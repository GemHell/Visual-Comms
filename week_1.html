Setting Up Your Github Account

  • Create a free https://github.com/ account and use Github Pages to create a site for the course.
    • Pages Quickstart Guide
  • Apply the concepts from the tutorial chapters above to create an index page for your site with your name in <h1> tags and your creative statement in <p> paragraph tags.
    • At the end of the syllabus, there are examples and tips for writing a creative statement.
  • Make a link to a second page with an image and a link back to the home page.
  • Check to make sure all of your links work.
  • Provide a link to your lab instructor using the method they designate.
